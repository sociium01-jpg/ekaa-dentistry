Why Bleeding Gums While Brushing Deserve Early Attention

A trace of bleeding when brushing is often brushed aside as normal, yet it is the earliest sign of gingival inflammation. Left untreated, sub-gingival tartar deepens gum pockets and weakens the bone supporting your teeth.

Modern ultrasonic scaling and diode laser disinfection clear bacteria gently in a single visit—without scalpels or sutures.
