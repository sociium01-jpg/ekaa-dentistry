Crowns & Bridges FAQs

Old PFM crowns contain a dark metal base beneath white ceramic. Upgrading to 100% metal-free Zirconia or E-Max eliminates the dark collar permanently.

If adjacent teeth are healthy and untouched, a single implant preserves them intact. If the neighbouring teeth already have root canals or large fillings, a 3-unit Zirconia bridge protects all three in 4 days.

Typically 3 to 4 working days from tooth preparation to final cementation.
