Gentle Child Dental Care

Protecting Milk Teeth & Guiding Adult Smiles

Primary molars hold the exact space needed for permanent teeth to erupt straight until age 12. We prevent childhood cavities before drilling is ever needed.

  • ✓Fluoride Varnish Therapy:Remineralizes soft developing enamel against sweet snacks and juices.
  • ✓Painless Pulpectomy & Kids Crowns:Saves deeply decayed milk molars with prefabricated pediatric caps.
  • ✓Space Maintainers & Habit Breakers:Gentle appliances for thumb-sucking, mouth-breathing, or early tooth loss.
