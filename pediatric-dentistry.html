Importance of Early Dental Visits

Regular early dental visits with a child dentist are the foundation of good oral health. These pediatric dental care appointments allow our dentists to monitor the development of your child’s teeth and jaws while identifying potential concerns at an early stage. Professional cleanings help remove plaque and tartar buildup, reducing the risk of cavities, which are common among children. To further protect young teeth, fluoride treatments may be recommended to strengthen enamel and prevent decay. Additionally, dental sealants are applied to the chewing surfaces of molars to protect them from food particles and bacteria.

At Ekaa Dentistry, we provide early dental visits for children, offering early orthodontic assessments to identify any alignment or jaw development issues.
