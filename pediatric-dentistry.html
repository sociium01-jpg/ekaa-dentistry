Why Primary (Milk) Teeth Matter So Much

Primary molars hold the exact space needed for adult teeth to erupt straight and guide healthy jawbone development until age 12.

Chipped or Knocked-Out Tooth on the Playground?

Kids Dentistry FAQs

Primary molars aren't replaced until age 10–12. Untreated decay causes painful abscesses, damages the developing permanent tooth bud underneath, and causes adjacent teeth to drift and block adult teeth.

Pediatric dental academies recommend a first "happy visit" around the child's 1st birthday or within 6 months of the first tooth erupting.

Just 5 minutes per tooth—with zero numbing injections and zero drilling. Your child can eat and drink normally right after the visit.
