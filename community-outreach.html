PREVENTION STARTS IN THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

Making Preventive Dentistry Accessible to Every Family

At Ekaa Dentistry, Dr. Chandni S Nair and our clinical team believe that nearly 90% of complex dental procedures can be avoided through timely awareness, proper brushing technique, and early childhood screening.

Every month, we partner with schools, residential welfare associations (RWAs) in Sree Prabhupada Township, Masjid Banda, Kondapur, and Gachibowli, as well as IT corporate campuses to host complimentary oral health checkups.
