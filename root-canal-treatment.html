Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment, also known as endodontic therapy, is a highly effective procedure designed to save severely infected or damaged teeth while alleviating dental pain. Although often misunderstood as a painful treatment, modern root canal therapy is safe, precise, and virtually painless when performed by experienced professionals. At our Kondapur dental clinic, Ekaa Dentistry, we provide advanced root canal treatment in Serilingampalle, Kondapur, and Gachibowli, with a focus on patient comfort, accuracy, and long-term results. Root canal therapy is recommended when the dental pulp—the soft tissue inside the tooth containing nerves and blood vessels—becomes infected or inflamed. This infection can occur due to deep tooth decay, repeated dental procedures, cracks in the tooth, or dental trauma.

When Is Root Canal Treatment Required?

You may require a root canal treatment at the Kondapur dental clinic if you experience: persistent tooth pain, sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling or tenderness in the gums, tooth discoloration, or pain while chewing. Early diagnosis is crucial as it helps prevent further infection and tooth loss, making endodontic therapy an important option to consider.

Root Canal Procedure at Ekaa Dentistry

At Ekaa Dentistry, a trusted Kondapur dental clinic, every root canal treatment follows a carefully planned and patient-centred approach: Comprehensive Evaluation The procedure begins with a thorough clinical examination supported by digital X-rays to assess the extent of infection and plan endodontic therapy accurately. Pain-Free Treatment A local anaesthetic is administered to numb the area, ensuring a comfortable and stress-free experience throughout the procedure. Removal of Infection Using advanced endodontic instruments, the infected pulp is gently removed. The root canals are meticulously cleaned and disinfected to eliminate bacteria. Sealing the Tooth The cleaned canals are filled with biocompatible gutta-percha, which seals the tooth and prevents reinfection while maintaining its strength. Crown Placement After Root Canal Treatment After the root canal procedure, the tooth is temporarily sealed. In most cases, a custom-made dental crown is placed during a follow-up visit to:

  • Protect the treated tooth
  • Restore natural appearance and function
  • Prevent fractures
  • Ensure long-term durability

The crown is carefully designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, supporting both function and aesthetics. Advanced Technology for Precise Root Canal Care Ekaa Dentistry uses modern dental technology to enhance accuracy and patient comfort, including:

  • Rotary endodontic systems
  • Digital imaging
  • Advanced sterilisation protocols

These techniques allow for faster treatment, improved precision, and predictable outcomes. Long-Term Care After Root Canal Treatment With proper care, a tooth that has undergone root canal treatment can last a lifetime. Patients are advised to:

  • Maintain good oral hygiene
  • Attend regular dental check-ups
  • Avoid excessive pressure on the treated tooth

Our team provides clear post-treatment instructions to ensure optimal healing and long-term oral health.

What is a root canal treatment? Root canal treatment is a dental procedure used to remove infected or inflamed pulp from inside a tooth, clean and disinfect the canals, and seal them to prevent reinfection, helping save the natural tooth.

Is root canal treatment painful? Modern root canal treatment is performed under local anesthesia and is generally comfortable. Most patients experience little to no pain during the procedure.

How long does a root canal procedure take? A root canal treatment usually takes one to two appointments, depending on the severity of infection and the tooth involved.

How long does a tooth last after root canal treatment? With proper oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, a tooth that has undergone root canal treatment can last many years or even a lifetime.

When is a dental crown needed after root canal treatment? A dental crown is often recommended after root canal treatment to protect the tooth, restore strength, and prevent fractures, especially for back teeth.

What happens if root canal treatment is delayed? Delaying root canal treatment can allow the infection to spread, potentially leading to increased pain, abscess formation, and eventual tooth loss.
