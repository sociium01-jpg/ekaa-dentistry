About Us

Dr. Chandni S Nair is the Founder, Clinical Director, and Lead Specialist at Ekaa Dentistry, a multispeciality dental clinic located in Kondapur, near Gachibowli, Hyderabad. With over 10 years of clinical experience, Dr. Chandni is a highly regarded Periodontist and Implantologist, known for her ethical, evidence-based approach and strong focus on long-term patient well-being. Her journey in dentistry is shaped by academic excellence, extensive corporate experience, and a deep commitment to community-driven oral healthcare.

Specialization: Periodontics & Implantology

Dr. Chandni’s core expertise lies in Periodontics and Dental Implantology, where she focuses on treating gum diseases, restoring lost teeth, and maintaining the health of the supporting bone and tissues. She believes that lasting dental outcomes can only be achieved when the foundation—healthy gums and bone—is addressed first. Her clinical focus includes:

  • Advanced periodontal therapy and laser-assisted gum treatments
  • Dental implant planning, placement, and long-term maintenance
  • Bone grafting and implant site preparation
  • Management of complex gum and implant-related conditions

Academic Excellence & Advanced Training

Dr. Chandni completed her Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Periodontics from S.R.M Dental College, graduating as one of the toppers of her batch. Her academic strength and clinical aptitude led her to pursue an advanced Fellowship from the prestigious Saveetha Dental College, where she gained extensive hands-on experience in advanced periodontal and implant cases.

Professional Experience & Insight from Corporate Dentistry

With over a decade of experience, Dr. Chandni has worked with leading dental corporate chains in Hyderabad, treating a wide range of patients and managing complex cases in high-volume clinical environments. This exposure gave her valuable insight into advanced systems, protocols, and patient expectations. At the same time, it helped clarify her belief that ethical dentistry must always place patient benefit above commercial considerations.

A Philosophy Rooted in Ethical, Evidence-Based Care

Drawing from her experience, Dr. Chandni founded Ekaa Dentistry with a clear purpose—to create a practice where treatment decisions are guided by science, integrity, and genuine patient need rather than commercial targets. At Ekaa Dentistry:

  • Treatments are conservative, transparent, and evidence-based
  • Natural teeth preservation is prioritized wherever possible
  • Patients are educated and involved in decision-making
  • Long-term oral health outcomes take precedence over short-term fixes

This philosophy differentiates Ekaa Dentistry from volume-driven corporate models and has earned the trust of patients across Kondapur and Gachibowli.

Community Commitment & Preventive Oral Health Awareness

Beyond clinical practice, Dr. Chandni is deeply committed to community oral health awareness. She actively participates in pro bono dental care initiatives and regularly conducts dental health camps in schools and gated communities across Hyderabad. Through these initiatives, she focuses on:

  • Educating children and parents about oral hygiene habits
  • Early identification of dental issues in growing children
  • Promoting preventive dental care to reduce long-term treatment needs
  • Creating awareness about the link between oral health and overall well-being

Dr. Chandni strongly believes that education and prevention are as important as treatment, and that accessible dental awareness can create lasting impact within the community.

Leadership & Collaborative Care

As Clinical Director, Dr. Chandni leads a multidisciplinary team of specialists at Ekaa Dentistry, ensuring comprehensive care under one roof. While she personally oversees periodontal and implant treatments, she collaborates closely with experts in orthodontics, endodontics, pediatric dentistry, oral surgery, and cosmetic dentistry to deliver holistic, well-coordinated care.

Beyond Dentistry – The Person Behind the Professional

Outside the clinic, Dr. Chandni is a passionate traveller, finding inspiration in exploring new cultures and perspectives. She also enjoys painting, which reflects her appreciation for detail, balance, and aesthetics—qualities that naturally translate into her clinical work. An avid dog lover, she values compassion and empathy in all aspects of life, traits that define her approach to both patients and community service.

Serving Kondapur, Gachibowli & Hyderabad’s IT Corridor

Dr. Chandni serves patients from Kondapur, Gachibowli, HITEC City, Serilingampalle, Madhapur, Hafeezpet, and surrounding areas. Ekaa Dentistry’s convenient location makes it an accessible choice for working professionals, families, and business travellers seeking ethical, specialist-led dental care.

A Vision Built on Trust, Integrity & Long-Term Care

Dr. Chandni S Nair envisions dentistry as a partnership—rooted in trust, education, and compassion. Through Ekaa Dentistry, she strives to redefine the dental experience by combining clinical excellence with ethical practice and community responsibility, helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles that last a lifetime.
