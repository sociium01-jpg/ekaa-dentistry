How We Practice

Four Pillars That Set Ekaa Apart

Every consultation follows a strict ethical checklist so you feel informed, relaxed, and in control.

Pillar 01 • Tooth Preservation

Save the Natural Tooth First

Extraction is always our last resort. Through microscopic root canal therapy, periodontal splinting, and laser pocket disinfection, we routinely rescue teeth that were elsewhere advised for removal.

Pillar 02 • Radical Transparency

Show, Explain, Then Treat

We display your intraoral photographs and digital radiographs on a chairside monitor and give you a written cost plan before touching a single tooth.

Pillar 03 • Anxiety-Free Care

Calm, Unhurried Appointments

We space our schedule deliberately so anxious adults, seniors, and first-time children never feel rushed in the dental chair.

Pillar 04 • Multi-Specialty Synergy

Visiting Super-Specialists Under One Roof

Alongside Dr.S.Kathiravan’s periodontal and implant expertise, our visiting panel includes senior Endodontists, Orthodontists, Pediatric Dentists, and Maxillofacial Surgeons—so complex cases are solved collaboratively in one clinic.
