Gentle Wisdom Tooth Removal & Rapid Healing

Horizontally tilted wisdom teeth trap food beneath the gum flap—causing recurrent jaw swelling (pericoronitis) and hidden decay on the healthy second molar next door.

  • ✓Impacted Wisdom Tooth Disimpaction:Micro-sectioning removes the tooth through a tiny window for minimal swelling.
  • ✓In-House OPG Nerve Mapping:Verifies exact distance from the inferior alveolar nerve before surgery.
  • ✓Socket Bone Preservation:Collagen & bone grafts preserve ridge width for future implants.
  • ✓TMJ Splints & Laser Frenectomy:Relief for jaw clicking, night grinding (bruxism), and lip/tongue ties.
