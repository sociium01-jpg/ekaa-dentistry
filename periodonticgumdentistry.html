Periodontic(Gum)Dentistry

Understanding Periodontal (Gum) Disease

Healthy gums are the foundation of strong teeth and long-term oral health. Gum disease, which includes conditions like gingivitis and periodontitis, affects the gums and the supporting structures of the teeth. If left untreated, gum disease can lead to tooth loss and may also be linked to systemic health conditions. At Ekaa Dentistry, we offer advanced periodontal treatment, including laser periodontal therapy, to effectively manage gum disease. This helps our patients maintain optimal oral health and confidently proceed with treatments such as dental implants and cosmetic dentistry when needed.

Advanced Laser Periodontal Therapy

Periodontal treatment begins with a thorough assessment of gum health, especially when it comes to detecting gum disease. Our dentists examine the gums for signs of inflammation, bleeding, recession, and the formation of periodontal pockets. This detailed evaluation enables early diagnosis and allows us to plan precise treatments, including advanced options like laser periodontal therapy, tailored to the severity of the condition.

Scaling & Root Planing for Gum Disease

For moderate to advanced gum disease, laser periodontal therapy provides a modern, minimally invasive solution. At Ekaa Dentistry, we utilize laser technology in our periodontal treatment to precisely remove infected gum tissue while preserving healthy tissue. This innovative approach offers several benefits, including: Reduced discomfort and bleeding, faster healing and recovery, minimal tissue damage, and improved treatment outcomes. Laser therapy is a preferred option for patients seeking effective gum treatment with enhanced comfort.

Cosmetic Gum Treatments

In addition to addressing gum disease, Ekaa Dentistry provides advanced cosmetic periodontal procedures to enhance smile aesthetics, including laser periodontal therapy for effective treatment of gum issues, gum contouring to improve gum symmetry, and crown lengthening for patients with excess gum tissue. These periodontal treatments not only create a balanced, natural-looking smile but also support overall oral health.

Periodontal Maintenance & Long-Term Care

Following periodontal treatment, including advanced options like laser periodontal therapy, periodontal maintenance programs are essential to prevent the recurrence of gum disease. Regular follow-up visits and professional cleanings are crucial for maintaining gum health and are as important as preventive dental care throughout life. Patients also receive personalized guidance on maintaining healthy gums at home, which includes:

  • Proper brushing and flossing techniques
  • Use of antimicrobial mouth rinses
  • Lifestyle and oral hygiene recommendations

Early intervention, including treatments such as root canal therapy when necessary, helps prevent complications related to gum disease and supports long-term dental stability.
